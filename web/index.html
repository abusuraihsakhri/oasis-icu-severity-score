Oxford Acute Severity of Illness Score

Compute the published OASIS point score from complete first-day ICU data. No account, network service, or patient identifier is required.

Research and educational use. OASIS is a population-derived severity score, not a bedside intervention or treatment directive. The original mortality calibration may not generalize to other cohorts. Do not enter identifying information.
